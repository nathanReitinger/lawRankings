How this is computed

Each specialty journal sits in one field of the subject tree (the Berkeley Technology Law Journal in technology law and policy, the Journal of Criminal Law and Criminology in criminal procedure, and so on). A faculty member's articles in a field's journals are counted, whole for co-authors, in the years chosen; short pieces are left out when "Skip short pieces" is on. "Focus" is the share of the person's specialty-journal articles that are in this field. Articles are credited to the school where each author teaches now. Articles in flagship law reviews, online editions and other general journals, and in the few generalist policy journals the subject tree places in no field, are not counted here. The tags next to names on the rankings page come from the same counts over all years (a field with at least 2 articles and a quarter of the person's specialty-journal articles).
