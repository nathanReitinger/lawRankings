Law School Rankings: FAQs

What is this site?

This site provides rankings and analytics on law school faculty scholarship output. Users can filter results by year, specialty, and more.

How are rankings calculated?

Rankings are based on the total number of scholarly publications by full-time faculty, weighted by journal prestige and specialty relevance.

Which journals are included?

We include all top law reviews and specialty journals from U.S. law schools, filtered and weighted according to specialty categories. The full list is available on the site.

Can I download the data?

Currently, we do not offer downloads, but we plan to provide CSV exports and API access in future versions.

Who built this?

This project was built by an interdisciplinary team of law and data science researchers who wanted to make scholarly output more transparent.

How can I suggest a correction?

You can email us at corrections@example.com with corrections or suggestions.
